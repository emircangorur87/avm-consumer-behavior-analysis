# Privacy and Authorship Note

## Privacy

- Student numbers and other unnecessary identifiers were removed from the portfolio edition.
- Raw respondent-level survey data are not included.
- The repository contains only aggregated findings and privacy-safe visuals.

## Authorship

The original research was completed as an academic team project at Istanbul University. This portfolio edition is curated in Emircan Görür's repository to demonstrate the team's research process and the analytical methods used. It does not claim that the original project was completed by one person.

If individual contribution details are added later, they should describe only work that can be accurately attributed and should not overstate sole ownership.
